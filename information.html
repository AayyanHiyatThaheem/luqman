INSIDE THE INDUSTRY

Truck Dispatching: From Load to Delivery

Truck dispatching is the process of coordinating freight movements between drivers, brokers and customers. A dispatcher helps organize loads, confirm shipment details, communicate schedules and maintain important records.

Our sample beginner program introduces students to load boards, route planning, rate negotiation basics, broker communication and freight documentation. Practical exercises help learners understand how these activities connect in a typical dispatching workflow.

What a dispatcher typically does

  1. Review available freight and shipment requirements.
  2. Discuss load details and rates with brokers.
  3. Coordinate pickup, delivery and route information.
  4. Keep drivers updated and organize shipment paperwork.

Dispatch Desk Note

Beginner tip: Always check pickup and delivery appointments, equipment requirements and shipment details before confirming a load. Clear communication can prevent avoidable confusion.
